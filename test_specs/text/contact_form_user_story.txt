Title: Guest sends a contact message

Description:
As a guest visitor,
I want to send a message through the contact form,
So that I can ask the hotel team a question before making a booking.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Guest can open the home page.
- Guest can find the contact form.
- Guest can enter a name, email address, phone number, subject, and message.
- Guest can submit the contact form.
- Guest sees a success confirmation after the message is sent.
- Contact message should not be submitted when required fields are missing.
- Invalid email format should show validation feedback.

Test Steps:
- Navigate to the home page.
- Locate the contact form.
- Fill in valid contact details.
- Submit the contact form.
- Verify that a success confirmation is displayed.
- Try submitting the form with missing required fields.
- Verify that validation feedback is displayed.
